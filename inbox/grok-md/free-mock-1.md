Text Solution

The passage mentions that in the previous mining cycle, China's growth drove the demand for commodities like coal and iron ore. The passage highlights that the appetite for copper from "green" industries, such as the increasing sales of electric vehicles, will provide almost half of the additional demand for copper in this decade. This emphasis on critical minerals and the demand for copper in the energy transition distinguishes the current cycle of mining mergers from the previous one.

The passage focuses more on the shift towards critical minerals and the demand for copper in the energy transition as the differentiating factor.


Option D: This choice is incorrect because the passage states that the previous mining cycle was characterized by China's demand for coal and iron ore. It does not mention that this higher demand for coal and iron ore is a differentiating factor in the current mining cycle.
