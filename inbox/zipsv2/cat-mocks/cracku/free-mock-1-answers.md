A

The increased valuation of mining firms, making acquisitions a more attractive option.

C

The emphasis on shareholder returns and balance sheet improvement by mining companies.

The higher demand for coal and iron ore driven by China's economic growth.

Text Solution

The passage mentions that in the previous mining cycle, China's growth drove the demand for commodities like coal and iron ore. However, in the current cycle, there is a shift in focus towards critical minerals, particularly copper, which will play a central role. The passage highlights that the appetite for copper from "green" industries, such as the increasing sales of electric vehicles, will provide almost half of the additional demand for copper in this decade. This emphasis on critical minerals and the demand for copper in the energy transition distinguishes the current cycle of mining mergers from the previous one.

However, this factor alone does not differentiate the current cycle of mining mergers from the previous one. The passage focuses more on the shift towards critical minerals and the demand for copper in the energy transition as the differentiating factor.


Option D: This choice is incorrect because the passage states that the previous mining cycle was characterized by China's demand for coal and iron ore. It does not mention that this higher demand for coal and iron ore is a differentiating factor in the current mining cycle.

Hence, Option B is the correct choice.

We have four terms in |a-b|+|b-c|+|c-d|+|d-a| that we need to individually maximize so as to get the overall maximum value. But since the terms overlap, we have to take the extreme values possible for the four numbers a, b, c, d, which are 1, 2, 8, and 9, and place them such that there is maximum difference possible.

There are 3 beakers A, B, and C having nitric acid of concentration 18%, 16%, and 21% respectively. Harish mixed 120 ml of A and 160 ml of B. If he needs the concentration of the final solution to be in the range of 17-19%, then what is the sum of the maximum and minimum amount of C that he can add to the solution of A and B?

Correct Answer: 310
Skipped

11
Text Solution

Harish will add the minimum amount of C when he needs a solution of 17%.
Let x be the minimum amount of C he needs to add to the solution.
Thus,
0.18*120 + 0.16*160 + 0.21x = 0.17(280+x)
Thus, 18*120 + 16*160 + 21x = 17(280+x)
2160+2560+21x = 4760+17x
=> 4x = 40
Thus, x = 10 is the minimum amount of C he needs to add.
He will add the maximum amount of C when he needs a solution of concentration 19%.
Let y be the maximum amount of C that Harish can add.
Thus,
0.18*120 + 0.16*160 + 0.21y = 0.19(280+y)
18*120 + 16*160 + 21y = 19(280+y)
4720+21y = 5320+19y
2y = 600
=> y = 300 is the maximum amount of C that can be added.
Hence, x+y = 10+300 = 310 is the required answer.
