CAT 2026 VARC 66 PYQs

Verbal ability

Jumbles, summary, odd one out.

01

Sheet

Eight questions, often TITA. RC first; VA in leftover 10–12 minutes. Two 90-second TITA jumbles beat one 5-minute stall.

Jumble. Opener = most general, no stranded pronoun, no “however / this / such.” Lock pairs (noun\(\to\)pronoun, term\(\to\)definition, problem\(\to\)solution). Chronology and logic connectors pin the rest.

Summary. Complete and unpadded. Kill narrower-than-paragraph, broader-than-paragraph, hedge-to-certainty.

Odd one out. Neighbour topic or different agent/time. Remaining three must jumble.

Para completion. Last sentence licensed by what is already there. A witty new essay is wrong.

Trap. The interesting sentence as opener. CAT openers are boring topic sentences.
Move. For TITA jumbles, write the four letters in order; don’t permute all 24 if two pairs are locked.
