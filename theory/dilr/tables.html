Copy the grid. Unknowns as letters. Row/column totals are equations — write the tightest first. “At least / at most / unique” stay inequalities until forced. Stop when the asked quantity is determined; CAT will not always need the full fill.

Round-robin of \(n\): games \(= n(n-1)/2\). Total wins \(=\) total losses \(=\) games (no draws). Points: read the scoring (2/1/0 vs 3/1/0). A team plays \(n-1\) games; wins \(\le n-1\).

\[\sum \text{wins} = \#\text{games},\qquad \sum \text{points} = (\text{pts per game})\times\#\text{games}\]

Matrix: dash on the diagonal. Fill forced W/L from “A beat everyone except B”, then the points column. Two parallel groups + a final is two tables, not one.

Networks at CAT scale: enumerate. Max flow is usually a 6-node picture, not Ford–Fulkerson theatre.

Trap. Assuming 2 points for a win. Assuming no draws. Completing the table when Q1 only needs a comparison of two forced cells.
Move. Pick a tournament if \(\le 6\) names and one scoring sentence. Pick a table if two totals are given and numbers are small integers.
