P&C, probability, sets

Counting, Bayes at CAT depth, Venn.

01

Sheet

P&C. \(n! = n(n-1)\cdots 1\). \(^nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}\). \(^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}=\,^nC_{n-r}\). \(^nC_r + \,^nC_{r-1}=\,^{n+1}C_r\).

Permutations of multiset: \(n!/(n_1!n_2!\cdots)\). Circular: \((n-1)!\) if rotations are the same; necklace with flips: \(n!/2n\) for \(n\ge 3\) distinct (careful: CAT usually just \((n-1)!\)).

Number of functions \(A\to B\): \(|B|^{|A|}\). Injective: \(^nP_{|A|}\) if \(|B|=n\ge|A|\). Onto: inclusion-exclusion \(n!\,S(k,n)\) or \(\sum_{i=0}^n (-1)^i \binom{n}{i}(n-i)^k\).

Probability. \(P=\frac{\#\text{favourable}}{\#\text{total}}\) equally likely. \(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\). Independent: \(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\). Conditional \(P(A|B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)\). Bayes: \(P(A|B)=\frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}\).

Binomial: \(P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}\). At CAT, \(n\le 8\) usually. Expected value of a count is linearity — you do not need independence.

Sets / Venn. \(|A\cup B\cup C|=\sum|A|-\sum|A\cap B|+|A\cap B\cap C|\). “Only \(A\)” \(= |A|-|A\cap B|-|A\cap C|+|A\cap B\cap C|\).

From CAT 2017–2020.

  • Stars and bars with caps. Identical erasers to kids, each at least 1, at most 3: set \(y_i=x_i-1\in\{0,1,2\}\), \(\sum y=3\), four variables. Inclusion-exclusion or listing (CAT-scale). Pens: Amal \(\ge 1\), Bimal \(\ge 2\), Kamal \(\ge 3\), 8 identical: shift \(a'+b'+c'=8-6=2\), non-neg, \(\binom{2+2}{2}=6\).
  • Integer solutions of \(x-y-z=25\) with upper caps. Let \(y'=y\), etc., or shift \(x=25+y+z\) and apply the three upper bounds; count the lattice rectangle that remains.
  • Three points among 5 diameters plus centre, forming a triangle. Total \(\binom{11}{3}\) minus collinear triples: each diameter with O is one line (\(\binom{3}{3}=1\) per diameter? A,O,B collinear — 5 such triples). Also no three endpoints of distinct diameters are collinear through O except those. \(\binom{11}{3}-5=160\)? 165-5=160.
  • 4-digit from \{0,2,3,4,6\}\), no repeat, divisible by 6 (even and digit-sum \(\div 3\)), no leading 0. Casework on last digit even, then the rest, skip 0 first.
  • Venn, “everyone who studies P also studies H or E.” Only-P is empty. 10 in the triple intersection, 20 in H\(\cap\)E only. \(|H|=|E|\), universe 74. Fill the seven regions; the empty only-P is the key.
  • Only-burger max/min. 105 pizza, 134 burger, 200 people. Only burger \(= |B|-|P\cap B|\). Intersection between \(\max(0,105+134-200)=39\) and \(\min(105,134)=105\). Only burger between \(134-105=29\) and \(134-39=95\). 93 is inside, 96 is not.
  • Symmetric difference \(A\Delta B=(A\cup B)\setminus(A\cap B)\) is associative. Compute element-wise on a 10-element universe, or note \(x\) ends in the result iff it sits in an odd number of the four sets.
  • Juniors 43, seniors 51, girl-girl junior matches 153 \(=\binom{g_j}{2}\) so \(g_j=18\). Boy-boy senior 276 \(=\binom{b_s}{2}\) so \(b_s=24\). Then boy-girl matches = junior mixed \(+\) senior mixed.
  • At least two subjects, all-three 18, maths 23, physics 25, min chemistry. Everyone has at least two, so the 18 triple already count toward each. Remaining maths 5 must pair with C or P; remaining physics 7 similarly. Min C is 18 plus the leftover who cannot go to MP only — 20 in the paper.
  • 3-digit with at least one repeated digit: 900 total 100–999, minus \(9\times 9\times 8\) strictly distinct (first 1–9, then rest avoiding used). 900-648=252.
Trap. Arranging people with two particular together: glue as one, \(\times 2\) for internal order. Complementary counting (total − bad) beats casework. Probability “at least one” is \(1-P(\text{none})\). Only-X bounds come from inclusion-exclusion on the complement, not from “looks plausible.”
Move. Write the sample space size first. If order doesn’t matter, C; if it does, P. For Venn, fill the innermost intersection first.
