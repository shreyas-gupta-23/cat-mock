CAT 2026 Arithmetic 7 PYQs

Ratio and proportion

Chain ratios, partnership, componendo, ages.

01

Sheet

A ratio is a scale. CAT adds one linear constraint that pins the scale, or chains two ratios on a common term.

Chain \(A:B=a:b\), \(B:C=c:d\): make \(B\) common \(\mathrm{lcm}(b,c)\).

\[\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}\iff ad=bc\]

Componendo / dividendo (and both): \(\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}\Rightarrow \frac{a+b}{a-b}=\frac{c+d}{c-d}\) if \(a\ne b\). Invertendo, alternendo as needed when the stem is a nested \((a+b)/(a-b)\).

Continued proportion \(a,b,c\): \(b^2=ac\). Duplicate ratio of \(a:b\) is \(a^2:b^2\) (areas); triplicate \(a^3:b^3\) (volumes).

Partnership. Profit \(\propto\) capital \(\times\) time. If times equal, \(\propto\) capital only.

Coins / ages. Value \(= 1\cdot n_1+2\cdot n_2+5\cdot n_5+\cdots\). Ages: the ratio changes because the difference is invariant (or both increase by the same \(t\)).

If \(kA=mB=nC=\lambda\), then \(A:B:C = \frac{\lambda}{k}:\frac{\lambda}{m}:\frac{\lambda}{n}\).

From CAT 2017–2020.

  • Integer scale of a chain. \(a:b=3:4\) and \(b:c=2:1\) \(\Rightarrow\) make \(b\) common: \(6:8:4\). Sum is a multiple of 18. Options that are not multiples of the scale are dead.
  • Transfer. \((ak+x):(bk-x)=p:q\). Cross-multiply; \(x\) as a fraction of Lalitha’s original is \(x/(bk)\).
  • Same addend on both. \((a+x):(b+x)=p:q\). Then new/old of one person is \((b+x)/b\).
  • Two-year salary ratios. 2010 as \(6k:5k:7k\), 2015 as \(3m:4m:3m\). One known % hike pins \(m/k\), then the others’ hikes. The two ratios are not on the same scale.
  • Capital \(\times\) rate. Interest \(\propto c_i r_i\). Given one interest difference, the total interest is determined.
  • Two mix-ratios and two profits (tea A, B sold at 40): that is P&L plus alligation. CP of the mix \(=\mathrm{SP}/(1+\text{profit})\). Two mixes give two weighted CPs, then alligation (or two linear equations) for \(\mathrm{CP}_A:\mathrm{CP}_B\).
  • Review of three scores. Old: Rama \(=\frac1{12}(M+A)\). New: each \(+6\), and new ratio given. Translate ratio to actuals, subtract 6, feed the first equation.
Trap. Adding a constant to both parts does not preserve the ratio. Mixing two alloys in equal weight vs equal volume are different if densities differ — CAT usually means weight.
Move. Write \(A=ak\), \(B=bk\). Translate every English clause into an equation in \(k\) (or two scales, then glue on the overlap).

CAT 2017 Slot 2 QA Q11 MCQ Ratios
If a, b, c are three positive integers such that a and b are in the ratio 3 : 4 while b and c are in the ratio 2 : 1, then which one of the following is a possible value of (a + b + c)?
  1. A 201
  2. B 205
  3. C 207
  4. D 210

Choice C — 207
