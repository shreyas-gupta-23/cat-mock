CAT 2026 Arithmetic 17 PYQs

Profit and loss

False weight, successive discount, two-article.

01

Sheet

\[\mathrm{SP}=\mathrm{CP}\bigl(1\pm \tfrac{p}{100}\bigr),\qquad \mathrm{discount}=\tfrac{\mathrm{MP}-\mathrm{SP}}{\mathrm{MP}}\]

Markup \(m\%\), discount \(d\%\): profit % \(= m-d-md/100\).

Successive discounts \(d_1,d_2\): net off \(= d_1+d_2-d_1d_2/100\). Never add.

False weight. Claims to sell at CP (or at \(r\%\) profit) but uses \(w\) g as 1000 g:

\[\text{true profit %}=\frac{1000(1+r/100)-w}{w}\times 100\]

If \(r=0\): \((1000-w)/w \times 100\).

Two articles. Same CP, \(+x\%\) and \(-x\%\): net 0. Same SP, \(+x\%\) and \(-x\%\): net loss \(x^2/100\%\) of CP (product constancy on CP). Sold two at same SP, one profit \(p\%\) one loss \(p\%\): loss \(= (p/10)^2\%\) on total CP.

Buy \(n\) get \(k\) free, marked at \(m\%\) above CP: effective discount \(=\frac{k}{n+k}\times 100\%\) on MP, then chain with markup.

From CAT 2017–2020.

  • Retail / discount / profit together. \(\mathrm{SP}=\mathrm{RP}(1-d)=\mathrm{CP}(1+p)\). “15% off RP still 2% profit; want 20% profit” \(\Rightarrow\) sell at RP (compute: \(0.85\,\mathrm{RP}=1.02\,\mathrm{CP}\) so \(\mathrm{RP}=1.2\,\mathrm{CP}\)).
  • Common SP of two qualities. Good mangoes \(2x\)/kg, medium \(x\). Buy 80 kg good + 40 kg medium, sell all at \(0.9\times 2x\). Profit on mixed CP.
  • Destroyed stock, same profit amount. 60 toys at 40% off MP give 20% on CP. 10 burn. New discount on 50 so that profit rupees match, not the %.
  • Chain of dealers. Customer pays \(\mathrm{CP}\times 1.1\times 1.3\times 1.5\).
  • Two goods, two selling regimes. Pen at \(-5\%\) and book at \(+15\%\) net +7; pen \(+5\%\) and book \(+10\%\) net +13. Two linear equations in the two CPs.
  • \(n\) at \(+p\%\), \(m\) at \(-p\%\), net profit in rupees. Not the \((p/10)^2\) identity unless SP is the same. Here CPs are equal: net \(= p\%(n-m)\times\mathrm{CP}\).
  • Chained then compared. Two tables, same CP \(p\): +20% then +30% vs −20% then −30%. \((x-y)/p = 1.2\times 1.3 - 0.8\times 0.7\).
  • Alligation on overall profit. Desktop +20%, laptop −10%, overall +2% on 50000. Mix ratio of the two CPs is alligation on +20 and −10 toward +2.
  • Discount then extra 25% off the already-discounted price is successive, not 45% off. Back out labelled price from total SP and the 10% profit, then profit if no discount at all.
  • Wasted weight + raise MP by \(p\%\) to hit a target overall profit: write rupees in and rupees out, one unknown \(p\).
  • Also on this page: ages as % (Arun 40% of Barun, later 50% — difference invariant); nested savings 50% FD, 30% of rest stocks; admission % of girls vs boys; two commodities whose prices rise 20% and 12%; exam % of three people plus a fourth 84% of the same \(N\); 28% young, 65% literate, 25% of literates young — two-way table, % old among illiterate.
Trap. Profit on SP is not profit on CP. “Cost price of 12 = selling price of 10” \(\Rightarrow\) profit \(20\%\) on CP, not \(16.\overline{6}\%\) unless they asked on SP.
Move. CP \(=100\) unless two goods force two CPs. False weight: money in vs true quantity out.
