SI and CI

Effective rate, CI−SI, installments.

01

Sheet

\[\mathrm{SI}=\frac{Prt}{100},\qquad A_{\mathrm{CI}}=P\Bigl(1+\tfrac{r}{100k}\Bigr)^{kt}\]

\(k=1\) yearly, \(2\) half-yearly, \(4\) quarterly. Effective annual rate for half-yearly \(r\%\): \(r+\frac{r^2}{400}\).

Two-year CI−SI at \(r\%\): \(P(r/100)^2\). Three-year CI−SI: \(P\bigl((r/100)^3 + 3(r/100)^2\bigr)\).

Amount in 3 years / amount in 2 years at CI \(= 1+r/100\) (same \(P\)). Difference of CI for consecutive years is itself a CI on last year’s interest.

Installments. Present value of an annuity of \(A\) per year for \(n\) years at \(r\%\) CI: \(A\bigl(1-(1+r/100)^{-n}\bigr)/(r/100)\). CAT usually \(n=2\) or \(3\): \(P = \frac{A}{1+r/100}+\frac{A}{(1+r/100)^2}+\cdots\).

Population / depreciation use the CI multiplier (growth signed).

From CAT 2017–2020.

  • Two equal CI instalments. Loan \(P\), rate \(r\), two years: \(P=\frac{A}{1+r}+\frac{A}{(1+r)^2}\). First instalment is interest of year 1 plus some principal; the formula already encodes that.
  • On-lending. Borrow \(X\) at 8%, add \(Y\), lend \(X+Y\) at 10%. Net kept \(=\) Ankit’s interest on \(X\) gives one equation; the “\(X+2Y\)” variant gives the second.
  • Split SI at three rates. 15 lakh: some at 6%, rest 2:1 at 4% and 3%. One linear equation for the 6% slice.
  • CI annual + CI semi-annual vs SI. Amal: \(12000\times 0.08\) plus \(10000\bigl((1.03)^2-1\bigr)\). Bimal: \(P\times 0.075\). Equal interest pins \(P\).
  • Two SI clocks, staggered start. Veeru 10000 at 5% from \(t=0\); Joy 8000 at 10% from \(t=2\). Set amounts equal: \(10000(1+0.05t)=8000(1+0.10(t-2))\).
  • CI of 2 years at 5% minus SI of 3 years at 3%. \(P\bigl((1.05)^2-1 - 0.09\bigr)=1125\).
  • Half-yearly for 1.5 years is three applications of \(r/2\): \(P(1.05)^3=18522\).
Trap. “\(r\%\) per annum compounded quarterly for 9 months” is three applications of \(r/4\), not \(3r/4\) simple. SI and CI coincide for one compounding period.
Move. For 2 years, prefer CI−SI \(=P(r/100)^2\) over expanding binomials. For fractional years mixed SI/CI, split the last fraction as SI on the last CI amount if the problem says so — read the stem.
