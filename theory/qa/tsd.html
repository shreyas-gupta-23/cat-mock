Time, speed, distance

Harmonic mean, races, boats, trains, circular.

01

Sheet

\[d=vt,\qquad 1\,\mathrm{km/h}=\tfrac{5}{18}\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad 1\,\mathrm{m/s}=\tfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{km/h}\]

Relative: same direction \(|v_1-v_2|\), opposite \(v_1+v_2\). Trains: crossing each other, add lengths; pole/man, one length; platform, train+platform.

Equal distances: \(\bar v = \mathrm{HM}=2ab/(a+b)\). Equal times: AM. \(n\) equal stretches at \(v_i\): \(\bar v = n/\sum(1/v_i)\).

Upstream \(b-s\), downstream \(b+s\). Then \(b=\frac{d+u}{2}\), \(s=\frac{d-u}{2}\). Still-water time vs stream: extra time is not \(s\) itself.

Races. When A finishes \(D\), B has run \(D-g\) if A beats B by \(g\) metres. Time same. Ratio of speeds \(= D:(D-g)\). “Beats by \(t\) seconds”: convert to metres via B’s speed.

To meet: together they close the initial gap at relative speed. Opposite start, distance \(D\), meet after \(D/(v_1+v_2)\). From the same point and back, first meet at lcm of times or use relative.

Escalator / walkator: steps visible \(= (v_{\mathrm{you}}+v_{\mathrm{esc}})t\) in steps; two people give two equations in step-size.

Circular track length \(L\): time between meetings \(L/|v_1\pm v_2|\) (opposite vs same). First meeting at \(\frac{v_1}{v_1+v_2}L\) from A’s start if they start together opposite.

From CAT 2017–2020.

  • Late / early by the same clock. Walk 12 vs 15 km/h, 10 min late vs 10 min early: \(\frac{d}{12}-\frac{d}{15}=\frac13\) hour. Scheduled time is the midpoint of the two arrival times.
  • Two speeds, unknown scheduled time, asked a third speed. \(d/8\) and \(d/15\) give two arrival clocks; the 9:10\(\to\)10:00 slot is 50 min, so \(v=d/(5/6)\).
  • After they meet, times \(t_1,t_2\) to finish. \(\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{t_2}{t_1}}\). (Car 1 takes 45 min after meeting, car 2 takes 20 \(\Rightarrow\) \(v_2=v_1\sqrt{45/20}\).) Same formula when remaining times are 1 min and 4 min: speed ratio \(2:1\).
  • Same direction vs opposite, same pair. Towards each other meet in 1 h, both east meet in 7 h: \(v_1+v_2=350\), \(|v_1-v_2|=350/7\).
  • Races, two at a time. A beats B by 1 km in 10 km \(\Rightarrow\) when A runs 10, B runs 9. A beats C: compose the ratios. Three-horse: first beats second by 11 m and third by 90 m; second beats third by 80 m — let length \(D\), speeds \(D:D-11:D-90\) from the first sentence, then the second sentence is a consistency check that pins \(D\).
  • Car A beats B by 45, B beats C by 50, A beats C by 90. When A finishes \(D\), B has \(D-45\), C has \(D-90\). The B-vs-C statement is when B finishes \(D\), C has \(D-50\). Compose: \((D-45)/D \times (D-50)/D = (D-90)/D\).
  • Wheels. Distance \(=2\pi r \times\) revolutions. \(N_A=N_B+5000\), same distance, radii 30 and 40.
  • Equal-time vs equal-distance on three speeds. Amal 1/3 of time each; Bimal 1/3 of distance each. Amal’s avg is AM of speeds; Bimal’s is HM. Then % extra time.
  • Train crossing two walkers, same direction. \(L/(V-2)\) in 90 s, \(L/(V-4)\) in 100 s. Post time is \(L/V\).
  • 1/3 usual speed \(\Rightarrow\) 30 min late pins usual time: usual \(t\), slow \(3t=t+1/2\) so \(t=15\) min. Then the return with a 4 min stop is a new speed on leftover distance.
  • Opposite on a circle. First meet when A has done 60% \(\Rightarrow\) speed ratio 3:2. If A is back at P at 10:12 and the 10:00 meeting was not at P, A’s remaining 40% took 12 min, so A’s full period is 30 min. B’s remaining 40% of the track at that meeting gives B’s period the same way.
  • Two tangent circles, walkers on each. Periods \(T=2\pi r/v\). First reunion at A is LCM of the two periods (full rounds).
  • Three on one circle. Anil and Sunil meet at start when both have run integer laps: LCM of their lap times. Ravi’s distance in that time is \(v_R\times t\).
  • Motorcycles every minute. Cyclist A\(\to\)B in 60 min; 45 bikes reach B by 11. Doubling cyclist speed halves his time; count bikes whose travel-plus-start fits.
  • Bypass / half-way slow then extra 5 miles. Split the return into two segments; onward is one segment. One equation in the unknown half-distance.
  • Boat, double still-water speed, total time down 75%. Down+up times: \(\frac{d}{b+s}+\frac{d}{b-s}\) vs \(\frac{d}{2b+s}+\frac{d}{2b-s}=\frac14\) of original. Ratio \(b:s\).
Trap. Average of 40 and 60 on a to-and-fro is 48, not 50. Rest time is not motion. Headwind/tailwind is boat-stream in disguise.
Move. Convert units once. Draw a line with marks. Races: freeze the winner at the tape and read the loser’s position.
