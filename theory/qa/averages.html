Averages

Weighted, replacement, AM–GM–HM, cricket.

01

Sheet

\[\bar x = \frac{\sum x_i}{n},\qquad n\bar x = \text{total}\]

Join \(x\): new \(=\frac{nA+x}{n+1}\). Leave \(x\): \(\frac{nA-x}{n-1}\). Replace \(x\) by \(y\): change in average \(=\frac{y-x}{n}\).

Weighted. \(\frac{n_1A_1+n_2A_2}{n_1+n_2}\). Alligation if you need the mix ratio given the combined average.

AM–GM–HM for positives: \(\mathrm{AM}\ge\mathrm{GM}\ge\mathrm{HM}\), equality iff all equal. Two numbers \(a,b\): \(\mathrm{AM}=\frac{a+b}{2}\), \(\mathrm{GM}=\sqrt{ab}\), \(\mathrm{HM}=\frac{2ab}{a+b}\), and \(\mathrm{GM}^2=\mathrm{AM}\cdot\mathrm{HM}\).

Equal distances \(\Rightarrow\) average speed is HM. Equal times \(\Rightarrow\) AM of speeds.

First \(n\) naturals: \(\frac{n+1}{2}\). First \(n\) odds: \(n\). First \(n\) evens: \(n+1\). Squares: \(\frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}\).

Ages: average of a group \(+1\) after one year. A child born: add 0 (or newborn age) and increment \(n\).

From CAT 2017–2020.

  • Two leave, their average given as a fraction of the original average. Original total \(22A\). Two who leave average \(A/3\), so they take \(2A/3\). Remaining 20 have total \(22A-2A/3\), and the stem says the average of 22 increases by 2 when they leave — that increase is on the 20: \(\frac{22A-2A/3}{20}=A+2\). One equation in \(A\).
  • First \(k\) and last \(k\). Let \(n\) tests, overall \(T\). Drop first 10: avg \(+1\). Drop last 10: avg \(-1\). First-10 avg 20, last-10 avg 30. If the two blocks overlap you must write it; if \(n>20\) they are disjoint and \(T=10\cdot 20 + 10\cdot 30 + (n-20)\mu\). The two “drop” statements pin \(\mu\) and \(n\).
  • Cap a subgroup to maximise its average. Overall 38, 30 people \(\ge 51\), at most 39 younger. To max the young average, minimise the old total (all 51) and take as many young as the “at most” allows, pushing the constraint until integers break.
  • Two leave-one-out averages. Total of 21 other than G, total of 21 other than R, overall. Ramesh’s 82.5 is one known. Translate to totals; Gautam is the unknown.
  • 20 integers \(\le 5\), 30-integer mean 5, max mean of those 20. The other 10 are \(>5\) (or \(\ge 6\) if integers). To max the 20, minimise the 10’s excess over 5. Integers: 10 at 6, then the 20 sum to \(150-60=90\), mean 4.5.
Trap. Combined average of 40 and 60 is not 50 unless the counts match. “Average of averages” is a weighted mean with hidden weights.
Move. Always restore the total. Cricket “to raise average from \(a\) to \(b\) in \(k\) more innings” is a linear Diophantine on remaining scores with a cap if they mention a highest possible innings.

CAT 2017 Slot 2 QA Q6 MCQ Averages
The average height of 22 toddlers increases by 2 inches when two of them leave this group. If the average height of these two toddlers is one-third the average height of the original 22, then the average height, in inches, of the remaining 20 toddlers is
  1. A 30
  2. B 28
  3. C 32
  4. D 26

Choice C — 32
