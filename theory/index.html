CAT 2026 theory

Short sheets: every CAT-usable identity, none of the Class-10 filler. Practice is the real CAT 2017–2020 papers, classified the way 2IIM and Cracku split the syllabus — percentages / P&L / SI-CI, ratios-mixtures-averages, TSD, work, geometry, numbers, algebra, modern maths; DILR as sets vs tables; VARC as RC vs VA.

How to use this

  1. Open a sheet. If you cannot see why a formula is there, you do not know the topic yet.
  2. Exam mode (right rail) for a clean pass; Formulas for the slabs only.
  3. Every topic ends with the actual CAT 2017–2020 questions on that idea, tagged by paper and slot. Then sit a full paper under Papers.
  4. Order: arithmetic → algebra / numbers → geometry → modern maths. DILR is selection plus families. VARC is traps; there is no grammar syllabus.
