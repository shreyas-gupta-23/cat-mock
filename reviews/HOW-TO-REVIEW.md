# How to review a mock

Use this when a markdown attempt file is in the chat or in `reviews/`.

## Scoring already applied

The file’s score is final. Do not recompute it unless a line looks inconsistent with these rules:

- MCQ correct +3, MCQ wrong −1, blank 0
- TITA correct +3, TITA wrong 0, blank 0
- A marked question that has an answer still counts
- TITA `21`, `21.0`, and `21.00` match. A wrong letter does not
- Percentile in the file is a CAT 2025 ballpark, not All-India rank

## What to write

A short report, in this order:

1. Score, section scores, and whether any section is under a 92-percentile ballpark.
2. Mistakes grouped by topic, not by question order. For each group: the error (concept, reading, or arithmetic) and the formula or move that fixes it.
3. Blanks that were gettable versus blanks that were correct skips.
4. Three drills for the next week, naming the topic and the paper to redo. Do not invent a new question bank.

Cite questions as `section Qn` from the attempt file. Do not paste long passages or full option lists into the report.

## What not to do

Do not change the marking scheme. Do not treat the percentile as a rank. Do not recommend a coaching bundle from one attempt.
